# Retro-Sketch

[Live demo → https://vniharika35.github.io/retro-sketch/](https://vniharika35.github.io/retro-sketch/)

Retro-Sketch is a browser-based pixel-art editor built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No installation required—just open the page and start creating!

---

## Features

### 🎨 Solid Drawing  
- Pick any colour with the **Pen Colour** picker.  
- Click or click-and-drag across the grid to paint individual pixels.

### 🧹 Erase Tool  
- Click **Eraser** then click a painted pixel to reset it to the current background colour.  
- Automatically returns to draw mode after each erase.

### 📋 Colour Grabber  
- Click **Colour Grabber** then click any pixel to sample its colour into your pen.

### 🌈 Rainbow Mode  
- Toggle **Rainbow** to paint each pixel with a random hue—perfect for colourful effects.

### ☁️ Shade & 💡 Lighten  
- **Shade** darkens the existing pixel colour for shadows and depth.  
- **Lighten** brightens existing pixels for highlights and glow.

### 🏞️ Background Colour Picker  
- Change your canvas’s background colour at any time.  
- Only untouched (never-painted) pixels update, so your artwork stays intact.

### 🖼️ Resizable Grid (1×1 to 60×60)  
- Use the slider to adjust from **1×1** up to **60×60** cells.  
- Your existing art is preserved in the overlapping area; new cells fill with background colour.

### 🔲 Toggle Grid Lines  
- Show or hide the 1px borders between cells for a clean or outlined look.

### ↩️ Undo (up to 50 steps)  
- Click **Undo** to step backward through all recent actions—drawing, erasing, shading, even background changes.

### 💾 Save to Browser  
- Click **Save to Browser** to snapshot your grid into localStorage.  
- Reload the page and your last saved artwork will automatically reappear.

### 📥 Export as PNG  
- Click **Export PNG** to download your art as a high-resolution image (10× scale).

---

## How to Use

1. **Choose your Pen Colour** and start clicking or dragging on the grid.  
2. Switch tools at any time:
   - **Eraser** resets a single pixel.  
   - **Colour Grabber** picks up any pixel’s colour.  
   - **Rainbow / Shade / Lighten** modify how pixels are applied.  
3. **Adjust Grid Size** to zoom in or out on your art.  
4. **Change Background** to repaint untouched cells.  
5. Use **Undo**, **Save to Browser**, and **Export PNG** to manage your work.

Enjoy creating pixel art in seconds—no setup, no fees, just pure retro fun!
